# Contributor Levels

## Level 0: First-time Contributor

- Submitted at least 1 merged PR (documentation, examples, or fixes)
- No additional permissions beyond public repository access

## Level 1: Regular Contributor

- Submitted at least 5 merged PRs
- Active in Discussions
- Eligible for maintainer nomination

## Level 2: Module Maintainer

- Nominated by an existing maintainer
- Responsible for specific modules (docs, SDK, protocols)
- Can review and merge PRs within their module
- Participates in RFC reviews

## Level 3: Core Maintainer

- Appointed by the Project Steward
- Oversees the health of the entire project
- Votes on protocol RFCs
- Can veto changes that violate project philosophy

## Level 4: Project Steward

- Founder / original creator
- Holds reserved powers as defined in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md)
- Final decision authority on protocol, brand, and philosophy
