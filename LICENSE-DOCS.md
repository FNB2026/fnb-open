## Why CC BY-NC-SA?

FNB documents define a philosophical position on data sovereignty, AI ethics, and personal memory. They should be freely shareable and adaptable, but:

- **Attribution** ensures the original vision is traceable
- **NonCommercial** prevents the philosophy from being co-opted for commercial surveillance or data harvesting
- **ShareAlike** ensures derivative works remain in the same open spirit
