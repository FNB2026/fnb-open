# FNB Manifesto

Information feeds are consuming relationships. Recommendation algorithms are replacing personal judgment. AI is turning human memory, emotion, relationships, and choices into black-box inputs.

FNB rejects this direction.

FNB believes that the future of social should not be content distribution, highlight reels, algorithmic matching, or platform-driven re-categorization of people.

The future of social should return to:

- Who I am;
- What I have experienced;
- Who I have real relationships with;
- Which memories are worth keeping;
- Which inferences can be trusted;
- Which data can be revoked;
- Which relationships I should confirm — not have the machine decide for me.

FNB is a **local-first, user-owned, auditable, explainable, correctable personal memory operating system and AI social paradigm platform**.

---

FNB is not another social feed.

FNB believes that AI should not silently own, rewrite, or trade human memory and relationships.

Every inference should have evidence.  
Every relationship should be user-governed.  
Every memory should have a source.  
Every correction should be preserved.  
Every user should be able to export, revoke, and understand their data.
